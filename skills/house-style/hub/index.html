House style

Ink for the softwareBlue pen for the app's voice

Quiet, dense, legible tools in the tradition of Linear and native macOS apps, drawn by hand on top, after izm_ni and mmqz8. The rules live in SKILL.md and illustration.md; this page shows them.

Ink prints the software.
I write in pen.
01Principles

Principles

  • Colour is meaning. Status, diffs, links, danger. Structure is paper, ink and two greys.
  • Ink and pen. Data and controls print in ink. The app's own voice is drawn in one blue pen.
  • Loud and quiet. One display title per page over small mechanical print. Rows stay dense.
  • Line before fill. Outlined controls, hairline rows, no filled sidebar pills.
  • Controls stay controls. Solid, outlined or underlined, by importance. Hover fills with ink.
  • Text informs. Every word and every pen mark points at something true.
  • Both modes. Light is paper and ink; dark is the same print inverted.
  • Contrast floors. Body 7:1, secondary 4.5:1, marks 3:1, borders 1.5:1.
  • Motion smooths. 120 to 200 ms, ease-out, off under reduced motion.
  • Flat structure. One level of container; a card never holds cards.
02Palette

Palette

Six neutral roles do the structure; pen is the one hue the app writes with. Ratios are measured live against paper. Status colours come from Alice and mark lanes, stripes and badges only.

03Type

Type

Four voices, each with one job. Rampart One appears once per page. Hachi Maru Pop belongs to the pen layer and never sets labels, buttons or data.

04Buttons

Buttons

Importance sets the style: solid ink for the one main action, an offset shadow for a primary that repeats on every row, an outline for secondary, underlined text for the least. Pressing pushes the button into its shadow.

05Inputs

Inputs

Outlined fields on paper. Focus draws a pen ring; errors turn the border to the failed status and say what to fix in one line.

06Components

Components

Alice's real parts. Floating layers (dialogs, menus, toasts) get a hard ink shadow in place of a blur; everything else sits flat on the paper.

07Pen marks

Pen marks

The whole vocabulary of the pen layer. Each mark points at something real: a circle around the number that matters, a star beside what needs you, an arrow from a note to its subject.
